TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

Municipal Cemetery Management Platform – San Carlos and Cachapoal

Version 1.0 | Prepared from “EETT_Cementerio_2026_2.docx” (27 pages).

Purpose: convert the procurement specification into an implementation-oriented product and engineering specification for development, verification, deployment and handover.

1. Document basis, scope and interpretation

This document is derived from the supplied municipal technical specification. It reorganizes the source into a buildable product specification; it does not waive, replace or silently reinterpret the procurement requirements. Where the source is silent or internally ambiguous, the issue is explicitly marked for confirmation.

The source describes two independently managed municipal cemeteries (San Carlos and Cachapoal), a 15-month SaaS service beginning on the signed implementation acceptance date, and a migration from the Excel export “Base de datos CEMENTERIO.xlsx” with cutoff 31-03-2026.

1.1 Delivery outcomes

One 100% web application, responsive and usable in current Chrome, Edge and Firefox without plugins.

Independent cemetery records and configurable patios, pavilions, rows and numbering ranges.

Complete historical traceability for people, properties, burials, exhumations, rentals, ownership transfers, works, payments/income references and documents.

Instant bidirectional search, availability and reservation management.

Templated, numbered, tamper-evident PDF certificates with QR verification.

Immutable audit trail, role-based access, security controls, backup/recovery and data export.

Complete, reconciled migration of the specified source workbook and documented exceptions.

Operational readiness: training, manuals, support ticketing, monitoring and handover.

3. Functional modules and requirements

3.1 Cemetery and location catalog

Support separate cemetery records. Each cemetery contains patios and/or pavilions. Patios have name, rows and configurable numbering ranges; pavilions have name, rows and configurable niche-number ranges. Locations include type, number, row and parent patio/pavilion. Types include underground grave (simple or waterproofed), elevated grave, vault, mausoleum, common patio grave, rented niche and perpetual niche. Catalog edits and disabling must preserve historical links; never physically delete a location referenced by history. Display lists of patios and pavilions.

3.2 Property registry and complete property file

Register graves, vaults and mausoleums with location, owner, purchase date, income/order number and scanned order attachment. Every property must have a complete file showing current and historical owners, all burials and exhumations, works/repairs, transfers, rentals where applicable, linked income/order numbers, observations and documents. One property can contain multiple burial records. Disabling a patio/pavilion/location must not erase history.

3.3 Rental management

Register rented niches with location, rental period, tenant/lessee, linked burial, income/order number and supporting documents. Configurable periods must include 2 months, 5 years, 20 years and 50 years; historical data also includes 3-year rentals and perpetual records. Register common-patio grave rentals with grave number and row and link them to a burial. Produce rental certificates. List expired and upcoming rentals at 30/60/90 days, filterable by cemetery and pavilion, showing tenant contact details to authorized roles and exportable to Excel. Administrator dashboard displays counts.

3.4 Perpetual niche purchases

Register perpetual remains-niche purchase: location, deceased person's name and date of death, buyer full name, RUT, address, phone, linked income/order number and image/document. Generate a purchase certificate.

3.5 Burial / interment records

Capture deceased full name as atomic given names, paternal surname and maternal surname; RUT with Chilean check-digit validation for new records; age; cause of death; date of birth; date of death; burial permit attachment (image/PDF); responsible person's full name, RUT, address and phone; associated income/order number and attachment; target location and observations. Link burial to a grave, vault, mausoleum, common patio grave or niche. Cause of death visible only to Administrator and Administrative roles. Imported legacy records are marked “migrated” and exempt from new-record RUT validation.

3.6 Exhumations and transfers

Record source location, destination location, reason/type (internal transfer, other cemetery, judicial order, cremation, etc.), deceased person, date of death, exhumation/transfer date, requester, resolution number, related income/order references, supporting documents and observations. Link every movement to the original burial record. Preserve the full movement chain; exhumed persons remain searchable with status and current location where applicable.

3.7 Master/worker jobs

Record paid work performed within the cemetery, including headstone installation, repair and construction. Each job has work type, date, master/worker, payment/income reference, linked property and/or niche registration number, documents and observations. Show the work in the property's history. Master/worker directory is maintained separately.

3.8 Ownership transfers

For graves, vaults and mausoleums, record former owner, new owner, transfer date and supporting document. Preserve all historical owners and show current owner distinctly.

3.9 Attachments and observations

Every record-entry area has an observations field. Administrator configures which attachment types are mandatory for each record type. Legacy migrated records are exempt from mandatory attachment rules. Accept PDF/JPG/PNG only, maximum 10 MB each; malware scan on upload; store privately and serve only to authenticated authorized users. Minimum 50 GB included.

3.10 Certificates

Generate templates, not manually composed documents, for grave/vault/mausoleum, rental (niche/common-patio grave), burial/interment and perpetual niche purchase. Populate deceased, property/location, income/order number and dates automatically. Include issuer/signatory name and the user who generated/printed it. Generate non-editable PDF, preferably PDF/A. Unique sequential folio per certificate type and year. Reprint retains same folio. Cancellation requires mandatory reason and audit entry. Each PDF has a QR with random non-sequential token of at least 128 bits, pointing to HTTPS verification under municipal domain. Verification page shows certificate type, folio, issue date, status (valid/cancelled) and SHA-256 PDF hash; do not expose contact details. Allow JSON or CSV export of certificate metadata. An inserted signature image is not a validation mechanism. FirmaGob advanced electronic signature is optional/scored (8 points).

3.11 Availability and reservations

Search by grave/niche number and show available, occupied or reserved. Include cemetery, patio/pavilion, row and number. Register reservation with applicant, expiry date and supporting document. On expiry, location returns to available only after Administrator confirmation. Define and enforce state transitions; do not mark a location available if an active burial/lease prevents it.

3.12 Advanced search

Indexed bidirectional search. Person search by names, either surname or RUT returns exact cemetery, patio/pavilion, row, number, linked burial income/order number, current status and movement history. Location search by grave/niche number returns every matching location (numbers may repeat across patios/cemeteries), all persons associated and their statuses. Store given names, paternal surname and maternal surname separately. Autocomplete from 3rd text character and 1st numeric/RUT digit; max 20 suggestions plus paginated “view all”. Case-, accent- and diaeresis-insensitive; ñ equivalent to n; partial match across any name word and compound surnames; RUT with/without dots/hyphen. Filters: cemetery, property type, state and death-date range. Include exhumed/transferred people with status and current location. Results update in place without full-page reload or exporting data. One click opens person/property record. Search p95 ≤1 second with full migrated data and 30 concurrent users.

3.13 Reports and dashboard

At minimum: burials and exhumations; occupancy/availability by patio and pavilion; certificates issued/cancelled; workers' jobs; rentals expired/upcoming; income/order references by operation type. Filter by cemetery and date range; export Excel and PDF. Administrator home dashboard includes expired/upcoming rental counts.

3.14 Audit and record lifecycle

Append-only audit log that neither users, Administrators nor provider staff can edit/delete. Record actor user, RUT, role, source IP, exact timestamp synchronized to official continental Chile time, module, record ID, operation, before/after values for changed fields and success/failure. Events: login/logout including failures, lockouts, password changes, user/permission changes, create/edit/logical cancellation, certificate issue/reprint/cancel, export, attachment download, configuration changes and provider staff access. Protect with cryptographic hash chaining, immutable storage or equivalent; provide integrity verification. Administrator can filter/export by user/date/module/operation. Retain throughout contract and deliver in exit package. No physical deletion of business records; logical cancellation only, mandatory reason, visible in history.

3.15 Administration and configuration

Manage users/roles, cemetery catalogs, location types, rental periods, certificate templates, signatory details, required-attachment rules, reservation settings and other authorized parameters. Configuration changes are audited. Preserve old values and effective dates where changes affect history.

5. Architecture and engineering constraints

100% web SaaS; single centralized production database; concurrent multi-user operation; responsive UI; Chrome/Edge/Firefox support.

Cloud hosting provider must have current ISO/IEC 27001 certification. Declare provider, data and backup regions, database engine and all subcontractors with data access. Notify changes to provider/region/subcontractor 30 calendar days ahead.

Municipality supplies DNS for a municipal subdomain. All traffic HTTPS; TLS 1.2 minimum (TLS 1.3 preferred), TLS/SSL 1.0/1.1 disabled, HSTS and recognized CA. Qualys SSL Labs grade A or better throughout service.

Encrypt production database and attachments at rest with AES-256 or stronger. Encryption keys managed separately from data. Password hashing via Argon2id, bcrypt or equivalent.

Use OWASP ASVS Level 2 (current version) as security baseline and address OWASP Top 10. Before trial, deliver application/infrastructure vulnerability scan with no open critical/high findings.

Critical vulnerabilities fixed within 7 calendar days, or 72 hours if actively exploited; high within 30 calendar days. Municipality may conduct non-intrusive scans with 48 hours' notice.

File access must be authorization-checked; direct public attachment URLs are forbidden. Scan uploads for malware.

Concurrency: optimistic locking/version field or equivalent. If a second user edits a record already being edited/updated, warn and prevent silent overwrite; require explicit conflict resolution.

Performance: p95 search ≤1 second; p95 record open/save ≤2 seconds under full dataset and 30 concurrent users. Establish reproducible load-test scenarios and report percentiles.

All times use Chile continental official time for display/audit, with unambiguous storage (UTC internally plus timezone conversion recommended).

7. Data migration

Source workbook: “Base de datos CEMENTERIO.xlsx”, cutoff 31-03-2026; 18 sheets, 86,499 total rows, including 18,418 burial records and 21,716 property/purchase/rental/renewal records (40,134 principal records). Do not assume this workbook itself is attached to the current development task; the supplied file is the procurement specification.

Build repeatable import pipeline: source profiling, staging tables, mapping, validation, normalization, exception report, dry-run, approval and production import. Preserve source sheet and row for every migrated record and set migrated=true.

R1 RUT normalize to 12345678-K; validate DV. Invalid values remain with “RUT no validado” and exception; never discard.

R2 split names into given names, paternal and maternal surnames, preserving particles (de, del, de la, San, y). Ambiguous cases go to exceptions.

R3 parse Excel serial/text dates. Resolve two-digit years contextually; unparseable dates become blank with marker and original text preserved.

R4 map cemetery, patio/pavilion, row and number using municipal mapping; unmatched records become exceptions.

R5 detect duplicates by RUT or name + death date + location; do not auto-merge. Send to municipal decision list.

R6 never invent/infer missing data; retain blank with “sin dato en origen”.

R7 preserve source sheet and row; mark migrated.

R8 UTF-8, retain accents/ñ, trim extra spaces. Repair ? replacing Ñ only using municipality-approved name dictionary; unresolved cases are exceptions.

R9 any source field without destination mapping must be preserved in observations; no source data silently discarded.

R10 retain original workbook in platform read-only.

R11 treat DOB 1901-01-01, age 0 with no DOB, and dates before 1900 or after cutoff as missing/invalid according to R3/R6. Preserve NN as unidentified person.

R12 tmp_exhumaciones duplicates EXHUMACIONES and must not create duplicate events. exhu_lib rows absent from EXHUMACIONES (about 998) migrate as historical exhumations after municipal validation. BUSQUEDAS and Esquema are not data imports.

Deliver exception spreadsheet with sheet, row, rule and original value. Municipality resolves within 5 business days; vendor applies resolutions within 3 business days. All correction cycles included.

Reconciliation per sheet: migrated + exceptions + approved justified exclusions = source rows. Municipality samples 300 random records: zero errors in location, deceased name and death date; ≤1% errors in other fields. If failed, correct and sample again.

Municipality supplies workbook, catalogs, cemetery-sector mapping and missing code definitions within 3 business days after PO acceptance and NDA, via encrypted channel. Municipality delays suspend implementation timeline by same period.

Migration acceptance requires signed act, reconciliation report and resolved exception report. Delete external working copies within 10 business days after approval and provide deletion certificate.

13. Important clarifications before production

Source scope names “Cementerios Municipales de San Carlos y Cachapoal”; confirm whether Cachapoal is a separate cemetery/site under the same municipal authority and confirm its location catalog.

The procurement specification mentions “Cachapoal” but the detailed examples and training location emphasize San Carlos. Confirm users, cemetery-specific signatories and branding for each.

Confirm exact status transition rules for a location containing multiple burials, an active rental, an exhumed person, a reservation or a perpetual niche.

Confirm whether common patio graves and all property types share one location inventory model or need distinct subtypes.

Confirm whether rental renewal is a new rental transaction linked to the prior contract or an update to the existing rental; preserve full renewal history.

Confirm which records require which mandatory documents, and who can waive missing attachments for exceptional new records. Migrated records are explicitly exempt.

Confirm certificate folio format and whether folio sequence is global per certificate type/year or per cemetery; source says per type and year.

Confirm whether issuer/signatory and printing user are separate fields and whether a digital signature is required beyond the optional FirmaGob integration.

Confirm the municipality domain/DNS, certificate verification path, email/SMS channels, institutional contacts and Chile official time source.

Confirm handling of data not included in the workbook, including any legacy system tables not exported (tb_autopcias, tb_nom_firma, tb_usuarios, ubicacion_pertenece). Do not invent their contents.

Confirm the source workbook and municipal sector mapping are supplied separately; this uploaded procurement specification is not the actual data workbook.

Confirm retention after the 15-month contract and any legal retention obligations with the Municipality.
